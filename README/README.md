# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides business stakeholders with clear insights into sales, profit, customer segments, regional performance, and product performance. It enables data-driven decision-making through interactive visualizations and KPI monitoring.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to design an interactive business dashboard that helps organizations monitor key business metrics and make informed decisions using data visualization techniques.

- 👥 Customer Segment Analysis
- 🎛 Interactive Slicers (Region, Category, Segment, Order Date)

---

## 💡 Business Insights

-Business Insights

• Monthly sales demonstrate a positive growth trend, with peak performance observed during the final months of the year.

• The West region is the strongest revenue contributor, indicating higher customer demand in this market.

• Technology products outperform other categories and contribute the highest sales.

• Consumer customers account for the largest proportion of total sales, making them the primary target segment.

• Sales are concentrated among a limited number of high-performing products, suggesting opportunities for focused inventory planning and marketing strategies.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Recommendation

• Increase inventory for Technology products and focus marketing efforts on the Consumer segment in the West region to maximize future sales growth.
